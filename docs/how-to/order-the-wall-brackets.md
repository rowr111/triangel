# How to order the wall brackets

Three wall brackets are used to attach Triangel to a wall or to hang it from something. They also hold the Triangel out from the wall, leaving room behind it for the controller board and the cables. They are the same with or without the frame.

There are two kinds, depending on where the Triangel goes. You need 3 of one kind. All three corners use the same bracket.

## 1. Choose where the Triangel goes

### Flat on a wall

The bracket holds the Triangel 60 mm out from the wall, with one wall screw each.

### Across the corner of a room

The Triangel tips into the corner where two walls meet, with its point down into the corner and its top edge along the ceiling. The bracket's feet fold to sit against both surfaces at each corner, with a screw hole in each face, so you choose how many to use.

## 2. Upload it to JLC3DP

On [jlc3dp.com](https://jlc3dp.com), start a 3D printing quote and upload the STEP file. Choose:

- **Material:** MJF nylon (PA12)
- **Color:** black
- **Quantity:** 3

I suggest ordering at least one extra, in case anything goes wrong and to have a spare on hand.  Personally I also recommend ordering both types of brackets for versatility in mounting.

## 3. Add the insert

Turn on JLC3DP's threaded-insert service:

- **Insert:** M3x4x5, the longer one, since these brackets carry the Triangel's weight. The six-way and three-way brackets use the shorter M3x4x3.
- **How many:** 1 in each bracket
- **Drawing:** attach the bracket's insert drawing
